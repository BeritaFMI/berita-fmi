Gunung Kerinci dan Green Canyon Pangandaran: Panduan Alam dari Ketua Umum PB FMI

Mount Kerinci and Green Canyon Pangandaran: A Nature Guide from FMI's Chairman

Dokumentasi kunjungan Ketua Umum PB FMI di dalam gua kawasan Green Canyon Pangandaran. Documentation of FMI's Chairman visiting a cave in the Green Canyon Pangandaran area.

Ketua Umum PB FMI, Mayjen Mar (Purn.) Buyung Lalana, membagikan panduan dua destinasi alam: Gunung Kerinci dan Green Canyon Pangandaran. Kerinci, puncak tertinggi Sumatera (3.805 mdpl) di TNKS, ditempuh via Kersik Tuo dua hari satu malam dengan simaksi Rp15.000 dan pemandu Rp500.000–700.000 per kelompok. Green Canyon — Cukang Taneuh — menawarkan susur sungai, body rafting, dan gua di Cijulang dengan tiket Rp20.000 dan sewa perahu Rp150.000–200.000. Keduanya menuntut kesiapan: fisik di ketinggian, kewaspadaan di sungai.

FMI's Chairman, Maj. Gen. (Ret.) Buyung Lalana, shares a guide to two natural destinations: Mount Kerinci and Green Canyon Pangandaran. Kerinci, Sumatra's highest peak (3,805 m) in Kerinci Seblat National Park, is climbed via Kersik Tuo in two days and one night with a IDR 15,000 permit and guides at IDR 500,000–700,000 per group. Green Canyon — Cukang Taneuh — offers river cruises, body rafting, and caves in Cijulang with IDR 20,000 entry and IDR 150,000–200,000 boat rental. Both demand readiness: physical at altitude, vigilance on the river.
