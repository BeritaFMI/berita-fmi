FMI Teken MoU dengan TAAN Nepal

FMI Signs MoU with Nepal's TAAN

KATHMANDU, NEPAL - Federasi Mountaineering Indonesia (FMI) menandatangani Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) dengan Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal (TAAN) di Kathmandu, Nepal, Sabtu (31/10/2026). Penandatanganan berlangsung di Sekretariat TAAN di Maligaun, dalam pertemuan bilateral yang dihadiri pimpinan kedua organisasi. Delegasi FMI dipimpin Brigjen TNI (Mar) Sandy Muchjidin Latief, mewakili Ketua Umum PB FMI Mayjen TNI (Mar) Purn. Buyung Lalana, S.E.

KATHMANDU, NEPAL - The Federasi Mountaineering Indonesia (FMI) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal (TAAN) in Kathmandu, Nepal, on Saturday (31/10/2026). The signing took place at the TAAN Secretariat in Maligaun during a bilateral meeting attended by the leadership of both organisations. The FMI delegation was led by Brigadier General TNI (Mar) Sandy Muchjidin Latief, representing FMI Chairman Major General TNI (Mar) Ret. Buyung Lalana, S.E.

TAAN adalah asosiasi payung agen trekking di Nepal. Badan itu berdiri pada 1978 oleh sejumlah operator trekking yang menilai sektor ini tumbuh cepat tanpa aturan main yang jelas, sehingga perlu prinsip usaha yang sehat sekaligus kelompok penekan yang bisa menyuarakan kepentingan industri kepada pemerintah. Sampai hari ini TAAN menaungi ribuan agen trekking dan ikut mengurus jalur-jalur treking. Sebagian pekerjaan itu berupa upaya sukarela untuk membangun, merawat, dan memperkenalkan jalur, sisi yang jarang terlihat pendaki karena hasilnya hanya terasa saat jalur dalam kondisi layak.

TAAN is the umbrella association of trekking agencies in Nepal. It was established in 1978 by a group of trekking operators who felt the sector was growing fast without clear rules of the game, and that it needed sound business principles as well as a lobby group able to carry the industry's interests to the government. Today TAAN represents thousands of trekking agencies and takes part in managing trekking trails. Part of that work is volunteer effort to build, maintain, and promote routes, a side trekkers rarely see because the result only shows when a trail is in decent condition.

MoU ini menetapkan kerja sama kelembagaan jangka panjang antara kedua organisasi. Isinya: pertukaran keahlian teknis dan pengalaman lapangan, pelatihan bersama, lokakarya, dan seminar bagi orang-orang yang bekerja di sektor treking dan pendakian, serta pengembangan kapasitas lembaga di kedua negara.

The MoU sets out a long-term institutional partnership between the two organisations. It covers the exchange of technical expertise and field experience, joint training, workshops, and seminars for people working in trekking and mountaineering, as well as capacity building for both institutions.

Bagian lain menyangkut promosi. TAAN dan FMI akan menjajaki peluang memperkenalkan Nepal dan Indonesia sebagai destinasi gunung, treking, dan kegiatan petualangan di pasar masing-masing. Kerangka itu juga menyebut jejaring profesional antarfederasi dan antaragensi, pertukaran budaya, serta hubungan antarmasyarakat. Keselamatan gunung, kesadaran lingkungan, dan praktik pariwisata yang bertanggung jawab masuk sebagai butir yang disepakati.

Another part concerns promotion. TAAN and FMI will explore opportunities to present Nepal and Indonesia as destinations for mountains, trekking, and adventure activities in each other's markets. The framework also names professional networks between federations and between agencies, cultural exchange, and people-to-people relations. Mountain safety, environmental awareness, and responsible tourism practices are among the agreed points.

Dari sisi Nepal, TAAN dipimpin Presiden Sagar Pandey, didampingi Sekretaris Jenderal Sonam G. Sherpa. Pertemuan dibuka dengan sambutan resmi dari kedua pihak, lalu dilanjutkan pembahasan soal kemitraan kelembagaan dan wilayah kerja sama baru. Di sela pertemuan, TAAN menyerahkan buku Trail Guide Book of Nepal kepada delegasi FMI.

On the Nepali side, TAAN was led by President Sagar Pandey, accompanied by General Secretary Sonam G. Sherpa. The meeting opened with formal remarks from both sides, followed by discussion of the institutional partnership and new areas of cooperation. On the sidelines, TAAN presented its Trail Guide Book of Nepal to the FMI delegation.

Delegasi FMI dalam rangkaian kunjungan ini didampingi perwakilan Republik Indonesia di Nepal dan Bangladesh: Wakil Konsul Kehormatan RI untuk Nepal Upendra; Atase Pertahanan KBRI Dhaka Kolonel Inf. Imam Setiawan; Pelaksana Fungsi Sosial Budaya KBRI Dhaka Sahid Nurkarim; dan Pranata Informasi Diplomatik KBRI Dhaka Tomo Sudrajat. Kehadiran mereka menandai bahwa kerja sama antarfederasi ini berjalan sebagai bagian dari hubungan kedua negara, bukan urusan organisasi semata.

During the visit, the FMI delegation was accompanied by representatives of the Republic of Indonesia in Nepal and Bangladesh: Upendra, Honorary Vice-Consul of Indonesia for Nepal; Defence Attache of the Indonesian Embassy in Dhaka Colonel Inf. Imam Setiawan; Social and Cultural Function Officer of the Indonesian Embassy in Dhaka Sahid Nurkarim; and Diplomatic Information Officer of the Indonesian Embassy in Dhaka Tomo Sudrajat. Their presence marked that this cooperation between two federations runs as part of the relations between the two countries, not as a matter for the organisations alone.

Bagi FMI, kerja sama ini menyentuh sisi yang selama ini jarang dibahas di dalam negeri, yaitu tata kelola jalur sebagai layanan, bukan hanya sebagai lokasi pendakian. Nepal membangun sistem treking dengan agen berizin, pemandu terlatih, dan jalur yang dirawat terus-menerus. Indonesia punya bahan pembanding yang lain: gunung api di kawasan Ring of Fire, hutan tropis, dan jalur-jalur yang tersebar di bentang kepulauan. Di Indonesia, jalur pendakian umumnya diurus pengelola taman nasional bersama kelompok masyarakat dan federasi, dengan standar yang belum seragam antarkawasan.

For FMI, this cooperation touches a side rarely discussed at home: trail governance as a service, not merely a place to climb. Nepal has built a trekking system with licensed agencies, trained guides, and trails that are maintained continuously. Indonesia offers a different comparison: volcanoes in the Ring of Fire region, tropical forests, and routes spread across an archipelago. In Indonesia, climbing routes are generally managed by national park authorities together with community groups and federations, with standards that are not yet uniform across regions.

Kedua organisasi menyatakan akan menjalankan kesepakatan itu lewat program bersama, pertukaran profesional, dan kegiatan kolaboratif lain. Yang paling dinanti di Indonesia adalah turunannya: jadwal pelatihan bersama, bentuk pertukaran tenaga ahli, dan kegiatan pertama yang akan digelar.

Both organisations said they would carry the agreement out through joint programmes, professional exchanges, and other collaborative activities. What Indonesia will be watching for is the follow-through: a joint training schedule, the shape of the expert exchange, and the first activity to be held.

MoU dengan TAAN melengkapi rangkaian kesepakatan FMI di Nepal pekan itu, setelah kerja sama dengan Nepal National Mountain Guide Association dan Nepal Mountaineering Association. Bagi TAAN, kesepakatan ini menambah jaringan mitra di Asia Tenggara, kawasan yang pasarnya tumbuh untuk pendakian dan wisata alam.

The TAAN MoU completes FMI's series of agreements in Nepal that week, after cooperation with the Nepal National Mountain Guide Association and the Nepal Mountaineering Association. For TAAN, the agreement adds a partner in Southeast Asia, a region whose market for trekking and nature travel is growing.
