FMI Sumsel Buka Sriwijaya Mountaineering Course Batch 1 di Palembang

FMI South Sumatra Opens Sriwijaya Mountaineering Course Batch 1 in Palembang

BANDUNG, INDONESIA - Federasi Mountaineering Indonesia (FMI) Pengprov Sumatera Selatan membuka Sriwijaya Mountaineering Course 2026 Batch 1. Kelas itu digelar Jumat sampai Sabtu, 13 sampai 14 November 2026, di Ruang Pertemuan Wahana Outbound Belantara Keramasan, Palembang. Pendaftaran ditutup Kamis, 12 November 2026.

Materinya empat blok: manajemen pendakian, navigasi dan pemetaan manual, wilderness first aid, serta risk management. Alurnya lima tahap: learn, practice, simulate, assess, certify. Peserta dari organisasi pecinta alam dan penggiat alam membayar Rp 150.000 per orang, sudah termasuk kaus dryfit, sertifikat, makan siang, dan coffee break.

Laman resmi FMI Sumsel yang terbit 28 Oktober 2026 menyebut SMC sebagai ruang belajar bagi penggiat alam bebas. Tujuannya, menurut laman itu, meningkatkan wawasan, pengetahuan, dan kepedulian agar kegiatan di alam berjalan bertanggung jawab.

Sumatera Selatan punya Gunung Dempo di Pagar Alam, tujuan pendakian utama di provinsi itu. Dua dari empat materi SMC dipakai tepat di jalur seperti itu. Navigasi manual dipakai saat peta tidak lagi sejalan dengan tanda alam. Wilderness first aid dipakai saat korban harus ditangani sebelum bantuan datang.

Pengprov itu sudah menjalankan kerja serupa. Pada 2025, FMI Sumsel mendampingi warga Dusun Talang Kubangan, Desa Bandar Jaya, Kecamatan Dempo Selatan, Pagar Alam, dengan materi pengelolaan ekowisata berbasis masyarakat, teknik dan keselamatan pendakian, serta kepemanduan wisata.

Di SMC, empat materi itu disusun sebagai satu rangkaian. Manajemen pendakian mengatur perencanaan, logistik, dan waktu. Navigasi dan pemetaan manual mengandalkan peta, kompas, dan hitungan, tanpa aplikasi. Wilderness first aid menyiapkan penanganan korban dengan alat seadanya. Risk management menempatkan penilaian bahaya sebelum keputusan diambil.

Pola pelatihan seperti ini sudah berjalan di pengprov lain. FMI Jawa Timur menggelar diklat vertical rescue di Sidoarjo bersama mitra penyelamatannya, dan FMI bersama BRIN menggelar pelatihan remote and extreme environment di Cibinong. Yang ditawarkan SMC adalah jalur bertahap: lima tahap, dari belajar sampai sertifikasi.

Rangkaian lima tahap itu berakhir pada assess dan certify. Kelulusan ditentukan lewat penilaian di akhir, bukan hanya kehadiran di ruang kelas.

Laman itu ditutup dengan satu kalimat ajakan: belajar dari alam, memahami alam, dan menjadi penggiat alam bebas yang bertanggung jawab.

Pendaftaran dibuka lewat laman resmi FMI Sumsel. Informasi kegiatan juga disebar melalui akun Instagram @fmi.sumsel.

Kelas ini bukan pengganti latihan di medan. Materi manajemen pendakian dan risk management memberi kerangka yang bisa dipakai sebelum rombongan berangkat: siapa yang memutuskan, kapan harus berbalik, dan bagaimana keputusan itu dicatat.

Kursi Batch 1 dibuka untuk delegasi organisasi pecinta alam dan penggiat alam perorangan. Artikel terkait: Diklat Vertical Rescue FMI Jatim di Sidoarjo (084) dan pelatihan remote and extreme environment FMI bersama BRIN (070).

BANDUNG, INDONESIA - The South Sumatra chapter of the Federasi Mountaineering Indonesia (FMI) has opened Batch 1 of the Sriwijaya Mountaineering Course 2026. The class runs Friday to Saturday, November 13 to 14, 2026, at the meeting hall of Wahana Outbound Belantara Keramasan in Palembang. Registration closes on Thursday, November 12, 2026.

The material comes in four blocks: trek management, manual navigation and mapping, wilderness first aid, and risk management. The sequence has five stages: learn, practice, simulate, assess, certify. Participants from outdoor organisations and individual climbers pay IDR 150,000 each, which covers a dryfit shirt, certificate, lunch, and coffee breaks.

The official FMI South Sumatra page, published on October 28, 2026, describes the course as a learning space for people active in the outdoors. Its stated aim is to widen knowledge and awareness so that activities in nature are carried out responsibly.

South Sumatra has Mount Dempo in Pagar Alam, the province's main climbing destination. Two of the four subjects are used exactly on routes like that. Manual navigation matters when the map no longer matches the ground. Wilderness first aid matters when an injured climber has to be treated before help arrives.

The chapter has done similar work. In 2025, FMI South Sumatra assisted residents of Dusun Talang Kubangan, Bandar Jaya village, Dempo Selatan district, Pagar Alam, with community-based ecotourism management, climbing technique and safety, and tourist guiding.

In this course the four subjects form one sequence. Trek management covers planning, logistics, and timing. Manual navigation and mapping rely on map, compass, and calculation, without an app. Wilderness first aid prepares treatment with improvised gear. Risk management puts a hazard assessment before any decision.

Similar training already runs in other chapters. FMI East Java held a vertical rescue course in Sidoarjo with its rescue partner, and FMI with BRIN held a remote and extreme environment training in Cibinong. What this course offers is a staged path: five stages, from learning to certification.

The five stages end with assess and certify. Completion is decided by an assessment at the end, not by attendance alone.

The page closes with a line of invitation: learn from nature, understand nature, and become a responsible outdoor enthusiast.

Registration runs through the official FMI South Sumatra page. Information is also shared through the Instagram account @fmi.sumsel.

The class does not replace practice on the ground. The trek management and risk management sessions offer a framework to use before a group sets off: who decides, when to turn back, and how that decision is recorded.

Seats in Batch 1 are open to delegations from outdoor organisations and to individual climbers. Related reading: the FMI East Java vertical rescue course in Sidoarjo (084) and FMI's remote and extreme environment training with BRIN (070).
