Sidang Umum UIAA 2026 Ditutup di Kathmandu, 95 Delegasi dari 45 Negara

2026 UIAA General Assembly Closes in Kathmandu with 95 Delegates from 45 Countries

KATHMANDU, NEPAL - Sidang Umum UIAA 2026 ditutup di Kathmandu, Nepal, Sabtu 31 Oktober 2026. Sebanyak 95 delegasi dari 45 negara hadir. Bagi Nepal Mountaineering Association, ini kali ketiga menjadi tuan rumah, setelah 1982, tahun Deklarasi Kathmandu diterbitkan, dan 2011.

KATHMANDU, NEPAL - The 2026 UIAA General Assembly closed in Kathmandu, Nepal, on Saturday 31 October 2026. A total of 95 delegates from 45 countries attended. For the Nepal Mountaineering Association this was the third time as host, after 1982, the year the Kathmandu Declaration was published, and 2011.

Agenda pemungutan suara memuat amendemen sejumlah pasal dan lampiran Anggaran Dasar UIAA, yang disetujui. Sidang juga menerima laporan keuangan 2025, menyetujui rancangan anggaran 2027, serta membahas laporan Presiden Peter Muir dan Laporan Tahunan UIAA.

The voting agenda included amendments to several articles and annexes of the UIAA Articles of Association, which were approved. The assembly also received the 2025 financial results, approved the draft budget for 2027, and considered the report of President Peter Muir and the UIAA Annual Report.

Dua asosiasi diterima sebagai anggota baru dan berlaku 1 Januari 2027. Mountaineering and Sport Climbing Federation of the Kyrgyz Republic menjadi anggota penuh, sementara Club de Excursionismo, Andinismo y Camping dari Bolivia masuk sebagai anggota asosiasi. Saat ini UIAA menaungi sekitar 100 asosiasi di hampir 80 negara.

Two associations were admitted as new members with effect from 1 January 2027. The Mountaineering and Sport Climbing Federation of the Kyrgyz Republic becomes an active member, while Bolivia's Club de Excursionismo, Andinismo y Camping joins as an associate member. The UIAA currently represents around 100 member associations in nearly 80 countries.

Pemilihan menetapkan Heidi Harder dari DAV Jerman sebagai wakil anggota federasi terbesar. Young Hoon-Oh dari KAF Korea Selatan kembali menjabat wakil benua Asia untuk periode kedua, dan Nejat Akinci dari TDF Turkiye terpilih sebagai wakil umum. Di mahkamah federasi, Seyed Mohammadali Abdollahi dari Iran juga kembali untuk periode kedua. Etienne Grillot dari FFCAM Prancis menjadi ketua baru World Ice Climbing Board, menggantikan JoAnne Carilli Stevenson, dengan Mick Connor dari BMC Inggris sebagai anggota.

The elections confirmed Heidi Harder of DAV, Germany, as the Largest Federation Member Representative. Young Hoon-Oh of KAF, South Korea, was re-elected as Continental Representative for Asia for a second term, and Nejat Akinci of TDF, Turkiye, was elected as a General Representative. On the UIAA Court, Seyed Mohammadali Abdollahi of Iran was re-elected for a second term. Etienne Grillot of FFCAM, France, became the new Chair of the World Ice Climbing Board, succeeding JoAnne Carilli Stevenson, with Mick Connor of the British Mountaineering Council elected to the board.

Tiga tuan rumah berikutnya sudah ditetapkan. Sidang Umum 2027 berlangsung di Puerto Varas, Chile, 7 sampai 9 Oktober. Siprus menjadi tuan rumah 2028 melalui Cyprus Mountaineering Climbing and Orienteering Federation, dan Amerika Serikat pada 2029 melalui American Alpine Club.

The next hosts are already set. The 2027 General Assembly takes place in Puerto Varas, Chile, from 7 to 9 October. Cyprus hosts in 2028 through the Cyprus Mountaineering Climbing and Orienteering Federation, and the United States in 2029 through the American Alpine Club.

Tiga nama diangkat sebagai anggota kehormatan, yaitu Pierre Humblet dari Belgia, Josef Klenner dari Jerman, dan David Hillebrandt dari Inggris. Empat anggota kehormatan lain hadir di Kathmandu: Jordi Pons Sanjines, Frits Vrijlandt, Ang Tshering Sherpa, dan Injeong Lee.

Three new honorary members were elected: Pierre Humblet of Belgium, Josef Klenner of Germany and David Hillebrandt of the United Kingdom. Four other honorary members attended in Kathmandu: Jordi Pons Sanjines, Frits Vrijlandt, Ang Tshering Sherpa and Injeong Lee.

Di luar ruang sidang, rangkaian acara lebih panjang daripada sesi pemungutan suara. Simposium Keselamatan Gunung yang ketiga berfokus pada pengembangan pemuda, dengan pembahasan pencegahan dan penanganan penyakit ketinggian, pertolongan darurat di lapangan, keterampilan tali, perawatan peralatan, sampai penyelamatan improvisasi di medan curam dan gletser. KTT Perubahan Iklim ketiga, lokakarya strategi World Ice Climbing dengan arah 2034 dan 2038, serta dua sesi World Café melengkapi pertemuan.

Beyond the voting chamber, the programme ran far wider than the ballot. The third UIAA Mountain Safety Symposium focused on youth development, covering the prevention and treatment of altitude illness, emergency field care, rope skills, equipment maintenance and improvised rescue in steep and glacial terrain. The third Climate Change Summit, a World Ice Climbing strategy workshop looking towards 2034 and 2038, and two World Café sessions completed the meeting.

Delegasi FMI mengikuti rangkaian Sidang Umum di Kathmandu. Bagi Indonesia, keputusan di kota itu punya arti langsung: arah kerja federasi sampai 2031 dan pembahasan keselamatan di gunung akan menentukan bagaimana medan tropis, gunung berapi, dan jalur di kepulauan diperhitungkan dalam panduan internasional. Pengalaman Nepal, yang menerima 95 delegasi sekaligus menjalankan simposium lapangan di pegunungannya sendiri, menunjukkan pertemuan sebesar itu bisa dirangkai dengan latihan di medan nyata.

An FMI delegation joined the General Assembly series in Kathmandu. For Indonesia the decisions taken there matter directly: the federation's working direction to 2031 and its discussions on mountain safety will shape how tropical terrain, volcanoes and island routes are counted in international guidance. Nepal's experience, receiving 95 delegates while running field symposia in its own mountains, shows a meeting of that size can be paired with training on real ground.

Rangkuman resmi sidang dapat dibaca di theuiaa.org/review-2026-uiaa-general-assembly. UIAA menyatakan ulasan lengkap rangkaian acara dan laporan tahunan 2026 akan menyusul.

The official summary of the assembly can be read at theuiaa.org/review-2026-uiaa-general-assembly. The UIAA says a fuller review of the programme and its 2026 annual report will follow.
