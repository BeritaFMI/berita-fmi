Kalau K1 Bernama Masherbrum, Kenapa K2 Tidak Punya Nama?

If K1 Is Called Masherbrum, Why Does K2 Have No Name?

Di ketinggian 8.611 meter, K2 hanya 238 meter lebih rendah dari Everest. Selisih itu nyaris tidak berarti di ketinggian tersebut. Yang membedakan K2 adalah bentuknya: piramida batu dan es di Pegunungan Karakoram, di perbatasan Pakistan dan Tiongkok. Pendaki Amerika George Bell, anggota ekspedisi 1953, menyebutnya "gunung buas yang berusaha membunuh Anda". Julukan itu terpakai sampai sekarang: Savage Mountain.

At 8,611 metres, K2 stands just 238 metres below Everest. That gap means little at such an altitude. What sets K2 apart is its shape: a pyramid of rock and ice in the Karakoram range, on the border between Pakistan and China. American climber George Bell, a member of the 1953 expedition, called it "a savage mountain that tries to kill you". The nickname has stuck ever since.

Nama K2 lahir dari cara kerja survei, bukan dari cerita rakyat. Pada 1856, Thomas Montgomerie dari Great Trigonometrical Survey India Britania memetakan Karakoram dari Gunung Haramukh, sekitar 209 kilometer di selatan. Ia menandai dua puncak paling menonjol sebagai K1 dan K2, dengan K sebagai singkatan Karakoram. K2 tidak punya nama lokal yang lazim karena letaknya jauh dari permukiman, bahkan tidak terlihat dari Askole, desa terakhir menuju gunung. Chogori, dari kata Balti chhogo (besar) dan ri (gunung), pernah diusulkan sebagai nama lokal, dan versi Tiongkoknya, Qogir, dipakai resmi sampai hari ini. Nama Mount Godwin-Austen, untuk menghormati pengelana Henry Godwin-Austen, ditolak Royal Geographical Society. Kode abad ke-19 itu bertahan karena tidak ada nama lain yang benar-benar menggantikannya.

The name K2 came out of survey work, not folklore. In 1856, Thomas Montgomerie of the Great Trigonometrical Survey of British India mapped the Karakoram from Mount Haramukh, some 209 kilometres to the south. He marked the two most prominent peaks as K1 and K2, with K standing for Karakoram. K2 had no common local name because it sits far from any settlement and is not even visible from Askole, the last village on the approach. Chogori, from the Balti words chhogo (big) and ri (mountain), has been suggested as a local name, and its Chinese form, Qogir, is the official one today. The name Mount Godwin-Austen, honouring explorer Henry Godwin-Austen, was rejected by the Royal Geographical Society. The nineteenth-century code survived because no other name truly replaced it.

K1 justru puncak pertama yang dipetakan dalam survei itu, tetapi nasibnya berbeda. Penduduk setempat menyebutnya Masherbrum, dan nama itulah yang menempel. Artinya diperdebatkan: brum berarti gunung dalam bahasa Balti, sedangkan masher diduga dari mashadar (senapan lantak) karena bentuk puncaknya, atau dari masha (perempuan), sehingga muncul sebutan ratu puncak. Kode K1 dilepas begitu nama Masherbrum terkonfirmasi, kebalikan dari K2 yang bertahan karena tidak punya nama lain.

K1 was in fact the first peak mapped in that survey, yet it took a different path. Local people call it Masherbrum, and that name is the one that stayed. Its meaning is debated: brum means mountain in Balti, while masher may come from mashadar, a muzzle-loading gun, after the shape of the summit, or from masha, woman, giving the title queen of peaks. The K1 code was dropped once the name Masherbrum was confirmed, the reverse of K2, which survived because nothing else replaced it.

Tinggi menjelaskan sisanya. Masherbrum 7.821 meter, peringkat 22 dunia dan kesembilan di Pakistan. Angka itu masih di bawah ambang 8.000 meter, sehingga gunung ini tidak masuk daftar 14 eight-thousander yang diburu pendaki. Posisinya juga di luar arus utama: rute normalnya lewat Lembah Hushe di selatan, sementara semua lalu lintas treking dan ekspedisi 8.000-an masuk dari Gletser Baltoro di utara. Catatan pendakiannya tipis: setelah puncak pertama 1960, Himalayan Index hanya mencatat tiga pendakian tambahan dan enam kegagalan. George Bell, orang yang mencetuskan julukan Savage Mountain untuk K2, justru sampai ke puncak Masherbrum pada 1960 bersama Willi Unsoeld.

Height explains the rest. Masherbrum rises 7,821 metres, 22nd in the world and ninth in Pakistan. That is still below the 8,000-metre threshold, so the mountain never entered the list of 14 eight-thousanders that climbers chase. Its position is also off the main trail: the normal route runs from the Hushe Valley in the south, while all trekking and eight-thousander traffic enters from the Baltoro Glacier in the north. Its climbing record is thin: after the first ascent in 1960, the Himalayan Index lists only three further ascents and six failed attempts. George Bell, the man who coined Savage Mountain for K2, reached the top of Masherbrum in 1960 with Willi Unsoeld.

Rute paling ramai di K2, Abruzzi Spur, dipakai sekitar 75 persen pendaki dan seluruhnya berada di sisi Pakistan. Jalurnya melewati cerobong batu House's Chimney, tebing Black Pyramid, lalu Bottleneck: celah sempit tepat di bawah gletser gantung yang rapuh dan pernah runtuh. Sisi timur K2 belum pernah dipanjat karena lapisan es dan saljunya terlalu tidak stabil.

The busiest route on K2, the Abruzzi Spur, carries about 75 per cent of climbers and lies entirely on the Pakistani side. It passes the rock chimney known as House's Chimney, the Black Pyramid wall, and then the Bottleneck: a narrow couloir directly beneath a fragile, collapsing hanging glacier. K2's east face has never been climbed because its ice and snow formations are too unstable.

Percobaan serius pertama berlangsung 1902, dipimpin Oscar Eckenstein dengan Aleister Crowley di dalam tim, dan berhenti di 6.525 meter. Duke of Abruzzi mencoba pada 1909 lalu menyimpulkan K2 tidak akan pernah bisa didaki; tiga ekspedisi Amerika sampai 1953 juga gagal, dan yang terakhir menewaskan Art Gilkey. Puncaknya baru tercapai 31 Juli 1954 oleh Lino Lacedelli dan Achille Compagnoni, anggota ekspedisi Italia pimpinan Ardito Desio. Pendakian itu menyisakan perdebatan panjang: Walter Bonatti dan porter Hunza Amir Mehdi membawa tabung oksigen sampai 8.100 meter, lalu terpaksa bermalam tanpa tenda di atas 8.000 meter setelah kamp dipindahkan lebih tinggi dari kesepakatan. Mehdi kehilangan beberapa jari kakinya, dan fakta itu baru dibuka ke publik puluhan tahun kemudian.

The first serious attempt came in 1902, led by Oscar Eckenstein with Aleister Crowley in the team, and stopped at 6,525 metres. The Duke of the Abruzzi tried in 1909 and concluded K2 would never be climbed; three American expeditions up to 1953 also failed, and the last one killed Art Gilkey. The summit was finally reached on 31 July 1954 by Lino Lacedelli and Achille Compagnoni, members of an Italian expedition led by Ardito Desio. The ascent left a long controversy: Walter Bonatti and Hunza porter Amir Mehdi carried oxygen cylinders to 8,100 metres, then had to bivouac without shelter above 8,000 metres after the high camp was moved higher than agreed. Mehdi lost several toes, and the facts only became public decades later.

Wanda Rutkiewicz menjadi perempuan pertama di puncak K2 pada 23 Juni 1986. Musim dingin pertama baru terwujud 16 Januari 2021, ketika sepuluh pendaki Nepal yang dipimpin Nirmal Purja dan Mingma Gyalje Sherpa sampai ke puncak pada suhu sekitar minus 40 derajat Celsius; Purja satu-satunya yang tanpa oksigen tambahan. Pada 22 Juli 2022, 145 orang mencapai puncak dalam sehari dan 200 orang sepanjang musim.

Wanda Rutkiewicz became the first woman on the summit on 23 June 1986. The first winter ascent only happened on 16 January 2021, when ten Nepali climbers led by Nirmal Purja and Mingma Gyalje Sherpa reached the top at around minus 40 degrees Celsius; Purja was the only one without supplementary oxygen. On 22 July 2022, 145 people summited in a single day and 200 across the season.

Angka kematiannya tetap yang tertinggi di antara lima gunung tertinggi dunia. Sebelum 2021, K2 mencatat sekitar satu kematian untuk setiap empat pendaki yang sampai ke puncak. Per Agustus 2023, sekitar 800 orang pernah berdiri di puncaknya dan 96 orang meninggal dalam usaha itu. Bencana terburuk terjadi 1-2 Agustus 2008, ketika 11 pendaki tewas setelah runtuhan es memutus tali tetap di Bottleneck.

Its death rate remains the highest among the five highest mountains on Earth. Before 2021, K2 recorded roughly one death for every four climbers who reached the summit. As of August 2023, about 800 people had stood on top and 96 had died trying. The worst disaster came on 1 and 2 August 2008, when 11 climbers died after an icefall cut the fixed ropes in the Bottleneck.

Di sinilah urusan Indonesia masuk. K2 berdiri di Pakistan, dan di sana Alpine Club of Pakistan (ACP) adalah badan pendakian dan panjat nasional: berdiri pada 1974, berkantor di Islamabad, tercatat sebagai anggota Active UIAA sejak tahun yang sama, dan terdaftar sebagai federasi olahraga nasional di Pakistan Sports Board, badan olahraga pemerintah Pakistan. Lapisan di atas ACP karena itu pemerintah, bukan federasi lain. FMI dan ACP menandatangani nota kesepahaman di Kathmandu, Nepal, pada 31 Oktober 2026. Naskahnya menyebut ACP tidak akan mendukung permohonan izin pendakian, izin puncak, atau izin treking warga Indonesia tanpa surat rekomendasi resmi dari FMI, sementara FMI memeriksa kebugaran, kesiapan teknis, pengalaman ketinggian, dan asuransi sebelum keberangkatan. Ekspedisi bersama direncanakan menyasar jajaran Karakoram, Himalaya, dan Hindu Kush, dengan K2, Broad Peak, dan Nanga Parbat disebut sebagai contoh. Kesepakatan itu masih kerangka kerja, dan prosedurnya belum disusun.

This is where Indonesia comes in. K2 stands in Pakistan, and there the Alpine Club of Pakistan (ACP) is the national mountaineering and climbing body: founded in 1974, based in Islamabad, an Active UIAA member since the same year, and listed as a national sports federation by the Pakistan Sports Board, the government's sports authority. The layer above ACP is therefore the government, not another federation. FMI and ACP signed a memorandum of understanding in Kathmandu, Nepal, on 31 October 2026. The text says ACP will not support climbing, summit or trekking permit applications from Indonesian citizens without an official recommendation letter from FMI, while FMI checks fitness, technical readiness, altitude experience and insurance before departure. Joint expeditions are planned for the Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush, with K2, Broad Peak and Nanga Parbat named as examples. The agreement is still a framework, and its procedures have yet to be written.

Dalam catatan pendakian K2, belum ada nama pendaki Indonesia. Klaim pendaki Indonesia di puncak K2 yang sesekali beredar di media sosial tidak didukung catatan ekspedisi maupun pemberitaan kredibel. Capaian Indonesia di jalur yang sama tetap tercatat: Clara Sumarwati, perempuan pertama dari Indonesia dan Asia Tenggara di puncak Everest pada 26 September 1996, dan tim Indonesia Seven Summits Expedition Mahitala Unpar yang menuntaskan tujuh puncak tertinggi benua pada Oktober 2011. Di Asia Tenggara, Malaysia sudah mencatat nama: Mohd Khafiz Bachok menjadi orang Malaysia kedua yang sampai ke puncak K2 pada Juli 2023.

In the climbing record of K2, no Indonesian name appears. Claims of an Indonesian summit that surface now and then on social media are not supported by expedition records or credible reporting. Indonesia's achievements on the same path are on the books: Clara Sumarwati, the first woman from Indonesia and Southeast Asia to summit Everest on 26 September 1996, and the Indonesia Seven Summits Expedition Mahitala Unpar team, which completed the highest peak of every continent in October 2011. In Southeast Asia, Malaysia already has a name on K2: Mohd Khafiz Bachok became the second Malaysian to reach the summit in July 2023.

Jalan menuju K2 lazim dimulai jauh sebelum Karakoram: gunung 7.000-an, kemampuan membaca jendela cuaca, logistik tim, dan kesiapan medis di zona kematian. Di situlah standardisasi keselamatan dan kompetensi pendakian lebih menentukan daripada keberanian.

The road to K2 usually begins far from the Karakoram: 7,000-metre peaks, the ability to read weather windows, team logistics and medical readiness in the death zone. There, safety standards and climbing competence matter more than courage.
