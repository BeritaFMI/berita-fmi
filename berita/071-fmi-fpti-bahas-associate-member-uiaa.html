PB FMI dan PP FPTI Bahas Langkah FMI Menjadi Associate Member UIAA

FMI and FPTI Discuss FMI's Path to UIAA Associate Membership

Dokumentasi pertemuan PB FMI dan PP FPTI di Sekretariat FPTI, Jakarta Selatan, 4 September 2026. (Dokumentasi FMI) Documentation of the PB FMI and PP FPTI meeting at the FPTI Secretariat, South Jakarta, September 4, 2026. (FMI documentation)

Pengurus Besar Federasi Mountaineering Indonesia (PB FMI) menggelar pertemuan resmi dengan Pengurus Pusat Federasi Panjat Tebing Indonesia (PP FPTI) di Sekretariat FPTI, Jalan Cemara Nomor 19, Selong, Kebayoran Baru, Jakarta Selatan, Jumat 4 September 2026. Agenda utama pertemuan membahas proses FMI menjadi Associate Member UIAA (International Climbing and Mountaineering Federation), serta kerja sama kedua federasi dalam membangun dunia mountaineering Indonesia yang lebih baik.

PB FMI diwakili oleh Sekretaris Jenderal dr. Iqbal El Mubarak, Sp.B., Kepala Biro Litbang dr. Putro Setyobudyo Muhammad, MBL, dan Kepala Biro Hublu Zulfahmi Ramli. PP FPTI diwakili Sekretaris Umum Wahyu Pristiawan Buntoro, Wakil Sekretaris Umum Jaka Hidayat dan Dadang Sukandar, serta Kepala Bidang Hublu Florenciano Hendricus Mutter.

FPTI selaku tuan rumah menyatakan dukungan terhadap agenda FMI memajukan dunia mountaineering Indonesia. Sebagai tindak lanjut, kedua pihak akan mengagendakan pembahasan nota kesepahaman (MoU) antara FMI dan FPTI.

The Executive Board of the Indonesian Mountaineering Federation (PB FMI) held an official meeting with the Central Board of the Federasi Panjat Tebing Indonesia (PP FPTI) at the FPTI Secretariat, Jalan Cemara No. 19, Selong, Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta, on Friday, September 4, 2026. The main agenda was the process for FMI to become an Associate Member of UIAA (International Climbing and Mountaineering Federation), as well as cooperation between the two federations to advance Indonesian mountaineering.

PB FMI was represented by Secretary General dr. Iqbal El Mubarak, Sp.B., Head of the Litbang Bureau dr. Putro Setyobudyo Muhammad, MBL, and Head of the Hublu Bureau Zulfahmi Ramli. PP FPTI was represented by General Secretary Wahyu Pristiawan Buntoro, Deputy General Secretaries Jaka Hidayat and Dadang Sukandar, and Head of the Hublu Division Florenciano Hendricus Mutter.

FPTI, as the host, expressed support for FMI's agenda to advance Indonesian mountaineering. As a follow-up, the two federations will schedule discussions on a memorandum of understanding (MoU) between FMI and FPTI.
