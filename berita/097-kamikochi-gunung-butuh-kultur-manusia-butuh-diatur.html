Gunung butuh kultur, manusia butuh diatur

Mountains Need Culture, People Need Rules

BANDUNG, INDONESIA - Lembah Kamikochi di Chubu Sangaku National Park, Prefektur Nagano, Jepang, menerima 1,3 sampai 1,5 juta kunjungan per tahun. Angka itu stabil sejak 2004, tahun bus wisata dibatasi. Di musim libur dan musim daun merah, satu hari bisa 10 sampai 20 ribu orang. Yang masuk hanya bus, taksi, dan kendaraan berizin. BANDUNG, INDONESIA - The Kamikochi valley in Chubu Sangaku National Park, Nagano Prefecture, Japan, receives 1.3 million to 1.5 million visits a year. The figure has been stable since 2004, the year tour buses were restricted. During holidays and the autumn colour season, a single day can bring 10,000 to 20,000 people. Only buses, taxis, and permitted vehicles may enter. Private cars are not allowed.

Di tengah angka sebesar itu, sepasang pendaki Viv dan Kev mencatat apa yang membuat lembah ini tetap rapi. Catatan mereka diunggah di Instagram @vivchowvals pada 2 September 2026, setelah trek tiga hari dari hut ke hut di Northern Japan Alps. Isinya bukan soal pemandangan. Isinya soal tiga hal: jam, zona, dan aturan.

Amid those numbers, two hikers, Viv and Kev, wrote down what keeps the valley in order. Their notes were posted on Instagram at @vivchowvals on September 2, 2026, after a three-day hut-to-hut trek in the Northern Japan Alps. The subject was not the scenery. It was three things: time, zones, and rules.

Soal jam, catatan itu menyorot satu hal yang tidak biasa bagi pendaki Eropa. Di sana jam 15.00 biasanya jam mulai check-in. Di Jepang, jam itu justru batas terakhir. Tujuannya satu: pendaki tiba sebelum gelap supaya tim SAR tidak perlu naik malam-malam. Sebagian hut bahkan menolak pesanan kalau pendakinya baru mendarat di Jepang hari itu, karena takut terlambat sampai.

On time, the notes highlight something unusual for European hikers. There, 3 pm is usually the start of check-in. In Japan, it is the deadline. The purpose is simple: hikers arrive before dark so mountain rescue teams do not have to climb at night. Some huts even refuse a booking if the hiker landed in Japan that same day, for fear of a late arrival.

Hitungan jamnya ketat sampai ke meja makan. Slot makan malam dibagi per sesi, kadang sudah jam 17.00, dan waktu makannya 30 menit. Sarapan mulai jam 05.00. Check-out sebelum jam 08.00. Salah satu hut di jalur itu, Tokusawaen, menulis aturan yang sama di laman resminya: check-in mulai 14.00, check-out 08.30, dan pendaki diminta mengatur perjalanan supaya tiba paling lambat sekitar 16.00.

The clock is tight down to the dining table. Dinner slots are allocated by session, sometimes as early as 5 pm, and eating time is 30 minutes. Breakfast starts at 5 am. Check-out is before 8 am. One hut on that route, Tokusawaen, states the same discipline on its official page: check-in from 2 pm, check-out at 8.30 am, and hikers are asked to plan their walk so they arrive by around 4 pm at the latest.

Jalur di Kamikochi tidak dibagi berdasarkan selera. Ada empat zona resmi, dan tiap zona punya penggunanya sendiri. Zona 散策 untuk pengunjung biasa dengan sepatu sehari-hari. Zona 自然探勝 untuk pejalan dengan sepatu olahraga atau sepatu trekking. Zona トレッキング untuk pendaki. Zona 登山 untuk pendaki dengan sepatu gunung, di jalur yang dibangun khusus. Pembagian ini bukan brosur agen wisata. Itu kebijakan resmi Kementerian Lingkungan Jepang dalam dokumen 上高地ビジョン2026, halaman 14.

Trails in Kamikochi are not divided by taste. There are four official zones, and each has its own users. The 散策 zone is for ordinary visitors in everyday shoes. The 自然探勝 zone is for walkers in sports or trekking shoes. The トレッキング zone is for hikers. The 登山 zone is for hikers in mountain boots, on purpose-built trails. This division is not a travel brochure. It is official policy of Japan's Ministry of the Environment in the document 上高地ビジョン2026, page 14.

Papan namanya dipasang di batas zona. Salah satunya berbunyi: You are entering the Mountaineering zone. Di bawahnya ada peta zonasi dan syarat yang harus dipenuhi pendaki. Papan yang sama menyebut tiga hal yang jarang kita baca di jalur Indonesia: perlengkapan wajib sesuai jalur, rute dipilih sesuai kemampuan, dan biaya penyelamatan ditanggung oleh orang yang diselamatkan.

Signboards stand at every zone boundary. One of them reads: You are entering the Mountaineering zone. Below it are a zoning map and the conditions hikers must meet. The same board states three things rarely seen on Indonesian trails: equipment must match the route, the route must match the hiker's ability, and rescue costs are borne by the person rescued.

Ada lagi yang tidak tertulis di papan. Jalur teknis punya jam masuk terakhir, supaya sisa siang cukup untuk sampai. Di zona pendakian, helm dianjurkan. Beruang masih aktif di Pegunungan Alpen, jadi lonceng beruang dibawa di tas.

Some rules are not on the board. Technical trails have a last entry time, so that the remaining daylight is enough to reach the destination. In the mountaineering zone, helmets are advised. Bears are still active in the Alps, so a bear bell goes on the pack.

Kamikochi punya lima aturan inti ditambah dua, semuanya tertulis di laman resmi dan di papan jalur. Tidak memetik tanaman atau mengambil satwa. Tidak memberi makan satwa liar, termasuk monyet dan bebek. Tidak membuang sampah, semua dibawa turun. Tidak membawa hewan peliharaan atau spesies asing. Tidak menyimpang dari jalur. Tambahan dua: tidak bersepeda di jalur, tidak menerbangkan drone di dalam taman nasional.

Kamikochi has five core rules plus two, all published on the official site and posted along the trails. Do not collect plants or animals. Do not feed wild animals, monkeys and ducks included. Do not litter, everything comes back down. Do not bring pets or non-native species. Do not leave the path. The two additions: no bicycles on the trails, and no drones inside the national park.

Di hut, aturannya lebih ketat karena air bersih sangat terbatas. Pasta gigi dan sabun dilarang, supaya sumber air tidak tercemar bahan kimia. Tidak ada bak sampah di gunung, jadi semua yang dibawa naik harus turun lagi. Kamar tidur bersama wajib sunyi, karena besok semua orang harus bangun sebelum sarapan jam 05.00.

In the huts, the rules tighten because clean water is scarce. Toothpaste and soap are banned, so the water sources stay free of chemicals. There are no bins on the mountain, so everything carried up must come back down. Shared bedrooms must stay silent, because everyone has to be up before the 5 am breakfast.

Kenapa ini relevan? Indonesia punya 127 gunung api aktif, menurut catatan Badan Geologi per September 2025. Jalur ramai, pendaki pemula, dan jalur teknis sering bercampur di satu gunung yang sama. Tidak ada papan yang memberi tahu seseorang bahwa ia sedang masuk zona yang berbeda.

Why does this matter? Indonesia has 127 active volcanoes, according to the Geological Agency as of September 2025. Busy trails, novice hikers, and technical routes often mix on the same mountain. No signboard tells anyone that they are entering a different zone.

Kamikochi juga bukan taman yang aman dari gunung api. Di sisi selatannya berdiri Yakedake, gunung api aktif yang masih mengeluarkan uap, dengan rencana penanggulangan bencana yang disusun sejak 2011. Jadi cerita Jepang ini bukan cerita gunung sepi. Ini cerita gunung berapi yang dikunjungi sejuta orang lebih per tahun dan tetap bisa diatur.

Kamikochi is also not a park safe from volcanoes. On its southern side stands Yakedake, an active volcano that still releases steam, with a disaster plan drawn up in 2011. So the Japanese story is not a story about empty mountains. It is a story about a volcanic mountain visited by more than a million people a year that can still be managed.

Gunung butuh kultur, manusia butuh diatur. Aturan tanpa kultur hanya jadi papan yang dilewati. Kultur tanpa aturan hanya jadi niat baik yang menguap di jalur. Yang bisa ditiru dari Kamikochi bukan aturannya, karena aturan selalu ikut konteks. Yang bisa ditiru adalah kebiasaan menaruh aturan di tempat orang lewat, bukan di tempat orang mencari.

Mountains need culture, people need rules. Rules without culture become boards that people walk past. Culture without rules becomes good intentions that evaporate on the trail. What can be borrowed from Kamikochi is not its rules, because rules always follow context. What can be borrowed is the habit of putting rules where people pass, not where people search.
