Dua Ekspedisi Marinir ke Carstensz dan Aconcagua, di Masa Komando Buyung Lalana

Two Marine Corps Expeditions to Carstensz and Aconcagua Under Buyung Lalana

Banner Berita FMI: dua ekspedisi gunung tinggi Korps Marinir TNI AL, Carstensz Pyramid 2015 dan Aconcagua 2016. (Desain: Berita FMI) Berita FMI banner: two Indonesian Marine Corps high-altitude expeditions, Carstensz Pyramid 2015 and Aconcagua 2016. (Design: Berita FMI)

BANDUNG, INDONESIA - Korps Marinir TNI Angkatan Laut genap 81 tahun pada Minggu, 15 November 2026. Rangkaian peringatannya berisi upacara, bakti sosial, donor darah, dan bersih pantai. Di luar semua itu ada dua bab yang jarang dibuka lagi: dua ekspedisi gunung tinggi yang melibatkan prajurit Marinir.

Yang pertama ke Carstensz Pyramid di Papua, Agustus 2015. Yang kedua ke Aconcagua di Argentina, Maret 2016. Dua benua, dua medan, satu komando.

Ekspedisi Carstensz melibatkan 20 personel Marinir dan dipimpin Letnan Kolonel (Mar) Ferry Marpaung. Gunungnya setinggi 4.884 meter, puncak tertinggi Indonesia dan satu dari Seven Summits. Misinya bukan mengejar rekor: para prajurit mengantar Sabar Gorky, pendaki tunadaksa asal Solo, dan mengibarkan Merah Putih untuk HUT ke-70 Kemerdekaan Republik Indonesia.

Maret 2016 giliran Aconcagua, 6.961 meter, puncak tertinggi Benua Amerika dan salah satu yang tertinggi di luar Himalaya. Lima personel Marinir masuk ke dalam tim: Letnan Kolonel (Mar) Rivelson Saragih sebagai Komandan Satuan Tugas, Kopda (Mar) Sigit Purwoko, Kopda (Mar) Imam Rozikin, Praka (Mar) Erlando Brojosentiko, dan Praka (Mar) Didik Rudiyanto. Tim pendukungnya Asep Sumantri, Syatiri Ahmad, dan jurnalis Widya Victoria. Manajer ekspedisinya Dar Edi Yoga.

Dua gunung itu menuntut hal yang berbeda. Carstensz menuntut kemampuan teknis di batuan, dengan cuaca yang bisa berubah dalam hitungan menit dan akses yang bergantung pada penerbangan. Aconcagua menuntut tubuh bertahan di ketinggian hampir 7.000 meter, di udara tipis dan suhu di bawah nol. Satu gunung menguji keterampilan, satu lagi menguji ketahanan.

Catatan Dar Edi Yoga, yang juga manajer ekspedisi Aconcagua, menyebut disiplin prajurit sangat terasa di medan seperti itu. Kepatuhan pada prosedur, solidaritas, dan kesediaan saling menjaga menjadi modal, bukan pangkat dan jabatan. "Ketika berada di gunung, semuanya memiliki tujuan yang sama: menjalankan misi dengan penuh tanggung jawab dan kembali dengan selamat," tulisnya di Askara.co, 15 November 2026.

Dukungan pimpinan ikut menentukan. Saat dua ekspedisi disiapkan, Kepala Staf Angkatan Laut dijabat Laksamana TNI Ade Supandi dan Komandan Korps Marinir dijabat Mayor Jenderal TNI (Mar) Buyung Lalana. Buyung memegang jabatan Dankormar sejak 30 Maret 2015 sampai 15 Juni 2016, rentang yang persis melingkupi kedua ekspedisi itu.

Jejaknya tidak berhenti di sana. Buyung Lalana kini Ketua Umum PB Federasi Mountaineering Indonesia (FMI). Pada Rapat Kerja Nasional PB FMI 27 Juni 2026 ia menetapkan lima bidang strategis: pendidikan, penelitian, penjelajahan, olahraga prestasi, dan kebencanaan.

Bagi FMI, dua ekspedisi itu menyimpan pelajaran yang masih berlaku. Ekspedisi besar bukan urusan satu orang kuat, melainkan urusan manajemen: siapa yang memutuskan, bagaimana logistik diangkut, kapan rombongan harus berbalik, dan siapa yang menjaga kesehatan tim di lapangan. Carstensz sendiri masih jadi medan yang paling sering dibahas soal akses dan keselamatan, dari penamaan pos kesehatan (artikel 075) sampai penundaan penerbangan akibat kabut asap (artikel 076).

Delapan puluh satu tahun Korps Marinir, dan dua puncak itu tetap tercatat: satu di Papua, satu di Argentina. Dua-duanya bukan medan tempur.

Artikel ini disusun dari tulisan Dar Edi Yoga berjudul "81 Tahun Marinir: Kebanggaan Mendaki Carstensz dan Aconcagua Bersama Prajurit Petarung" yang terbit di Askara.co pada 15 November 2026, ditambah keterangan jabatan dari pemberitaan Berita FMI sebelumnya (artikel 057) dan data ketinggian gunung. Foto: Dok. Agoy, Askara.co.

BANDUNG, INDONESIA - The Indonesian Marine Corps turns 81 on Sunday, November 15, 2026. Its anniversary programme includes ceremonies, social service, blood drives, and beach clean-ups. Beyond all that sit two chapters that are rarely reopened: two high-altitude expeditions involving Marine soldiers.

The first went to Carstensz Pyramid in Papua in August 2015. The second went to Aconcagua in Argentina in March 2016. Two continents, two terrains, one chain of command.

The Carstensz expedition involved 20 Marine personnel and was led by Lieutenant Colonel (Mar) Ferry Marpaung. The mountain stands at 4,884 metres, the highest peak in Indonesia and one of the Seven Summits. The mission was not about a record: the soldiers were escorting Sabar Gorky, a climber with a disability from Solo, and raising the Merah Putih for the 70th anniversary of Indonesian independence.

March 2016 brought Aconcagua, 6,961 metres, the highest peak in the Americas and one of the highest outside the Himalayas. Five Marine personnel joined the team: Lieutenant Colonel (Mar) Rivelson Saragih as task force commander, Corporal (Mar) Sigit Purwoko, Corporal (Mar) Imam Rozikin, Private (Mar) Erlando Brojosentiko, and Private (Mar) Didik Rudiyanto. The support team consisted of Asep Sumantri, Syatiri Ahmad, and journalist Widya Victoria. Dar Edi Yoga served as expedition manager.

The two mountains demanded different things. Carstensz demands rock technique, with weather that can turn within minutes and access that depends on flights. Aconcagua demands a body that holds at almost 7,000 metres, in thin air and sub-zero temperatures. One mountain tests skill, the other tests endurance.

An account by Dar Edi Yoga, who also managed the Aconcagua expedition, describes how visible the soldiers' discipline was in such terrain. Adherence to procedure, solidarity, and a willingness to look after one another served as the real capital, not rank. "On the mountain, everyone shares the same goal: to carry out the mission responsibly and come back safely," he wrote in Askara.co on November 15, 2026.

Leadership support mattered. When the two expeditions were prepared, the Navy Chief of Staff was Admiral Ade Supandi and the Marine Corps commander was Major General (Mar) Buyung Lalana. Buyung held the post from March 30, 2015 to June 15, 2016, a span that covers both expeditions exactly.

His footprint did not stop there. Buyung Lalana now chairs the national board of the Federasi Mountaineering Indonesia (FMI). At the FMI national working conference on June 27, 2026 he set five strategic fields: education, research, exploration, competitive sport, and disaster response.

For FMI, those two expeditions carry lessons that still apply. A large expedition is not the work of one strong individual but a matter of management: who decides, how logistics move, when a team turns back, and who watches over the team's health in the field. Carstensz itself remains the terrain most often discussed for access and safety, from the naming of its health post (article 075) to flight delays caused by haze (article 076).

Eighty-one years of the Marine Corps, and those two summits remain on record: one in Papua, one in Argentina. Neither was a battlefield.
