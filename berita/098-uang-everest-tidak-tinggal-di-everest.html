Uang Everest tidak tinggal di Everest

Everest's Money Doesn't Stay on Everest

BANDUNG, INDONESIA - Musim semi 2026 menjadi musim tersibuk di Everest. Departemen Pariwisata Nepal mengeluarkan 492 izin pendakian, angka tertinggi yang pernah mereka catat. Padahal sejak 1 September 2025 harga izin naik 36 persen, dari 11.000 ke 15.000 dolar AS. Dari pintu izin saja Nepal mengumpulkan sekitar 7,4 juta dolar musim ini, dan untuk Everest sendiri kantor itu mencatat 1,07 miliar rupee atau sekitar Rp 129 miliar.

BANDUNG, INDONESIA - The spring 2026 season has become the busiest on Everest. Nepal's Department of Tourism issued 492 climbing permits, the highest number it has ever recorded. Yet since September 1, 2025 the permit has risen 36 percent, from USD 11,000 to USD 15,000. From permits alone Nepal collected about USD 7.4 million this season, and for Everest itself the department recorded NPR 1.07 billion, roughly IDR 129 billion.

Tarifnya bertingkat. Musim semi 15.000 dolar, September sampai November 7.500 dolar, Desember sampai Februari 3.750 dolar. Pendaki Nepal ikut naik, dari 75.000 menjadi 150.000 rupee. Ini kenaikan pertama sejak 2015. Direktur Jenderal Pariwisata Nepal, Narayan Prasad Regmi, hanya menyebut tarif lama tidak ditinjau, tanpa merinci bagian mana yang dipakai untuk gunungnya.

The tariff is tiered. Spring costs USD 15,000, September to November USD 7,500, December to February USD 3,750. Nepali climbers rose too, from NPR 75,000 to NPR 150,000. It is the first increase since 2015. Narayan Prasad Regmi, director general of the Department of Tourism, said only that the royalty had not been reviewed for a long time. He did not specify which part of it will be spent on the mountain.

Bagi pendaki, 15.000 dolar itu bukan biaya terbesar. Paket ekspedisi 2026 umumnya berkisar 40.000 sampai 100.000 dolar per orang. Sisanya untuk operator, penerbangan ke Lukla, oksigen, logistik, asuransi, dan upah tim pemasang jalur. Negara memegang pintu masuknya. Jalannya dipegang orang lain.

For climbers, the USD 15,000 is not the biggest cost. Expedition packages in 2026 generally run from USD 40,000 to USD 100,000 per person. The rest goes to operators, flights to Lukla, oxygen, logistics, insurance, and the wages of the teams that fix the route and carry loads to the camps. The state holds the gate. Other people hold the road.

Skalanya besar untuk ekonomi Nepal. Treking dan pendakian menyumbang lebih dari 4 persen produk domestik negara itu. Di lembah Khumbu, uang itu kelihatan di lodge Namche Bazaar, di pundak porter yang naik turun jalur, dan di warung-warung sepanjang rute.

The scale matters to Nepal's economy. Trekking and climbing contribute more than 4 percent of the country's domestic product. In the Khumbu valley the money shows up in the lodges of Namche Bazaar, on the backs of porters walking the trail, and in the tea houses along the route.

Kenaikan harga tidak menahan siapa pun. Musim ini 492 izin, lebih banyak dari 478 pada 2023 dan 468 pada 2025. Pendorongnya bukan tarif murah, tetapi sisi Tibet yang ditutup China, sehingga arus pendaki pindah ke selatan. Operator menambah kursi, pendaki tetap memesan. Di gunung setinggi ini, harga jarang jadi rem.

The higher price has not held anyone back. This season brought 492 permits, more than 478 in 2023 and 468 in 2025. The main driver was not a cheap tariff but China's closure of the Tibetan side, which pushed the flow of climbers south. Operators added seats, climbers kept booking. On a mountain this high, price is rarely the brake.

Yang tidak ikut naik adalah daya dukung jalurnya. Musim ini serak sepanjang 55 meter, lebar 37 meter, dan tinggi 28 meter runtuh sebagian di Khumbu Icefall. Empat orang cedera. Pada 2019, 354 pendaki mencapai puncak dalam satu hari dari dua sisi gunung, 223 di antaranya lewat Nepal pada 22 Mei. Mahkamah Agung Nepal sudah memerintahkan pembatasan berbasis daya dukung pada April 2024. Sampai musim ini, angka batasnya belum ada.

What did not rise is the carrying capacity of the route. This season a serac 55 metres long, 37 metres wide and 28 metres high partly collapsed in the Khumbu Icefall. Four people were injured. In 2019, 354 climbers reached the summit in a single day from both sides of the mountain, 223 of them through Nepal on May 22. Nepal's Supreme Court ordered capacity based limits in April 2024. As of this season, no maximum number has been set.

Sisa ekspedisi juga menumpuk. Lima kali kerja bersih-bersih tentara Nepal mengangkat 119 ton sampah dan 14 jenazah dari gunung. Diperkirakan 200 jenazah masih berada di sana. Pemerintah tidak pernah merinci berapa bagian dari uang izin yang dipakai untuk pekerjaan itu.

The leftovers of each expedition keep piling up. Five clean-up operations by the Nepali army have brought down 119 tonnes of rubbish and 14 bodies. An estimated 200 bodies remain on the mountain. The government has never detailed how much of the permit money pays for that work.

Indonesia memilih jalan lain. Sejak 3 November 2025, tarif masuk Rinjani naik dan jalurnya dibagi dalam beberapa kelas. Pendaki asing membayar Rp 250.000 per hari di jalur Sembalun, Senaru, dan Torean, sedangkan pendaki Indonesia Rp 50.000 di hari kerja dan Rp 75.000 di hari libur. Jalur Aikberik, Tetebatu, dan Timbanuh lebih murah. Dengan tarif itu, satu izin Everest setara lebih dari 1.000 hari pendakian di Rinjani.

Indonesia has taken another road. Since November 3, 2025, entry fees for Rinjani have risen and its routes are divided into classes. Foreign trekkers pay IDR 250,000 per day on the Sembalun, Senaru and Torean routes, while Indonesian trekkers pay IDR 50,000 on weekdays and IDR 75,000 at weekends. The Aikberik, Tetebatu and Timbanuh routes cost less. At that rate, one Everest permit equals more than 1,000 days of trekking on Rinjani.

Bedanya bukan cuma angka. Nepal menaikkan harga tanpa membatasi jumlah orang. Indonesia menaikkan tarif sambil mengelompokkan jalur. Dua-duanya menyisakan pertanyaan yang sama: berapa bagian uang pendaki yang kembali ke jalur, ke pos pencarian dan penyelamatan, dan ke warga di kaki gunung. Pertanyaan serupa berlaku untuk pendakinya. Membayar lebih mahal tidak membuat persiapan lebih matang, dan tidak ada tarif yang bisa menggantikan keputusan untuk berbalik.

The difference is not only the figure. Nepal raised its price without limiting the number of people. Indonesia raised its fees while sorting routes into classes. Both leave the same question open: how much of the climber's money returns to the trail, to the search and rescue posts, and to the people who live at the foot of the mountain. The same question applies to the climbers themselves. Paying more does not make preparation any better, and no tariff can replace the decision to turn around.

FMI sebelumnya membahas tata kelola serupa di Kamikochi, Jepang. Lembah itu menerima 1,3 sampai 1,5 juta kunjungan per tahun dan tetap teratur karena jam masuk, zona jalur, dan aturan yang ditempel di pintu masuk.

FMI has previously covered a similar approach in Kamikochi, Japan. The valley receives 1.3 to 1.5 million visits a year and stays orderly because of entry hours, route zones, and rules posted at the gate instead of filed away in a document.
